Design brief

Front pages, on Nocturne

What I read from the repo

Next.js 15 · React 19 · Tailwind 4 · shadcn new-york on the zinc base with lucide icons — which is exactly why it reads unbranded: zinc + new-york is the default look thousands of apps ship. Route groups are (auth) and (dashboard); domains are banks, transactions, categories, loans, investments, imports, settings. Notifications arrive over SSE, so the bell is live, not decorative.

What I'm changing

Nocturne's ground, Inter at 500, 8px radii, 0.7× density, outlined primaries, and rules that fade out at their ends. Every figure gets tabular numerals and Argentine formatting — $1.284.000,00, not $1,284,000.00. Money is the largest thing on any screen it appears on; chrome recedes.

Where it landed ↓

Nocturne is mono and forbids saturated fills; finance needs green and red. Settled: two semantic hues pinned to the accent's own lightness, used only as text, hairlines, sparklines and 14 % pill tints — never as a fill — and balances stay neutral because a balance is a fact, not a verdict. The full ramp and both modes are in 2a–2c; the production dashboard is 3a.

4Component library — build once, reuse everywhere
Why this exists

The nine boards in turn 3 are made of about forty distinct parts, and most of them appear on five or more pages. This is that inventory: every component with its props, its variants and the rule for using it, so a page becomes composition instead of new markup.

What counts as a component

Anything that appears twice with different data. Things that appear once — the net-worth hero, the import summary — stay page-level. Nocturne already ships buttons, tags, inputs, the segmented control, the table and the dialog; those are used, never rebuilt.

How to read it

4a is the catalogue, grouped by role, each specimen at production size with the props line above and the usage rule below. 4b is the reuse matrix — which page needs which part, and what to build first.

Reuse matrix · what to build first
4bComponent against page. Anything with five or more uses is infrastructure — build it first and the rest of the app is assembly.
Orden de construcción

Las cinco primeras filas se usan en las siete páginas: AppShell, TopBar, SideNav, Money y el juego de avisos. Con esas cinco y SplitLayout + KpiStrip queda armado el 60 % de cada pantalla; recién después conviene tocar gráficos.

Lo que no conviene componentizar

Los cinco gráficos son SVG a medida, no una librería: cada uno tiene una forma y unos datos. AccountCard y CreditCardCard viven solo en Bancos — no las generalices en una sola. Y Dialog ya lo trae el sistema de diseño.

3Production dashboard — the real feature set
What the backend actually serves

The gateway BFF (/api/v1/dashboard/data) fans out to three things only: transaction summaries per currency, YTD and month-to-date; loans; and upcoming payments. Investments are a separate service with a live IOL price feed. Notifications arrive over SSE. Everything on 3a maps to one of those.

Three things I'd add

Committed money — cuotas and loan instalments already owed over the next 12 months. Dual currency everywhere — the summaries are per-currency and there is a MultiCurrencyAmount component, so ARS with USD underneath, not a toggle. Price freshness — an IOL feed that can be 40 seconds or 40 minutes old must say which.

And the state nobody designs

The BFF returns OK | UNAVAILABLE per section and always answers 200 — so any card can arrive empty while its neighbours are fine. 3c specs that, plus loading and first-run empty, because otherwise it gets improvised in code.

Dashboards · desktop 1440 — one board per job
3aPanel — 1c's density and rhythm, rebuilt on the real domains: eight-route sidebar at the spec's 240px, per-currency figures, net worth as the hero chart, a switchable flow/cuotas chart beneath it, live holdings, upcoming payments and the SSE bell.

No — not for the logo. Nocturne's .lighten treatment is built for photographs and it's the right tool for the login screen and any marketing page, where a dark-ground image blends into the field. Rules that make it survive contact
Colour is not the only signal
Eight percent of your male users can't separate the green from the red. Every signed figure keeps its + / −, and direction pills keep the triangle glyph. Colour is the second cue, never the first.
Balances stay neutral
Only deltas and flows take green or red. A balance is a fact, not a verdict — colouring it makes every screen look like an alarm. This is what keeps the page calm at density.
Green and red never fill
They appear as text, as a 3px bar cap, as a sparkline stroke, and as a 14 % pill tint. No solid green buttons, no red rows. Saturated area belongs to nothing on this page.
Two grounds, one accent
Indigo is the only chromatic UI colour — selection, focus, links, charts. If a second hue shows up in chrome, something has gone wrong; green and red are semantic, not decorative.

Two open calls: (1) this ramp's 500 (#6d55da) is more saturated than Nocturne's #9184d9 — I can keep Nocturne's accent for chrome and use this only for depth, or replace it outright. (2) Light mode is specced but not built into 1a–1c yet. Say which dashboard wins and I'll build it in both modes.
